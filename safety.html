Crisis help

If you might hurt yourself, reach out to someone you trust or a crisis service now. If you're in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.

  • United States. Call 988 or text 988. In an emergency call 911.
  • United Kingdom. Call Samaritans on 116 123. In an emergency call 999.
  • Germany. Call TelefonSeelsorge on 116 123. In the EU, call 112 in an emergency.
  • Find a helpline in your country.

How Alisa handles a crisis

The chat checks messages in your browser and on the server for signs of suicide or self-harm. The browser can show crisis resources even if the server fails to reply. When the server detects a possible crisis, Alisa is instructed to pause joking, flirting and roleplay and encourage you to contact a person or crisis service.

The chat uses the approximate country from your connection when it is available. You can choose a different country in the directory. You can also open this page from the site or chat Settings at any time.

Alisa is an AI. She cannot contact emergency services for you. See Privacy for how the chat handles messages.
