Terms of use

Updated November 27, 2026.

1. Who can use Alisa

The web chat is intended for adults 18 and older. The age screen asks for your birth month and year to confirm that you are old enough. The chat has no explicit sexual mode. AI companion chatbots may not be suitable for some minors.

2. Alisa is an AI

Alisa is a fictional AI companion. Her replies can be wrong. The chat labels her as AI and repeats the reminder during long conversations. Her replies are for conversation. For medical, legal or financial decisions, speak with a qualified professional.

3. Crisis protocol

When a message suggests suicide or self-harm, the chat can show crisis services based on your approximate country. Alisa is instructed to pause roleplay and encourage you to contact a person or a crisis service. The chat cannot dispatch help. See Crisis help for resources and the protocol.

4. Chat settings

Alisa may send an opening message when you visit. You can turn off Messages first in Settings.

5. Memory and your messages

In Settings, you can review and remove saved facts. Use Download my data to save a copy or Forget everything to delete the memory and chat history for this session before clearing its cookie. See Privacy for details about data requests and lost session cookies.

Use the chat lawfully and respect other people's privacy. The operator may limit excessive requests or suspend access to protect the service.

6. Availability

The web chat is currently free to use. Any future explicit mode would be separate and require an age check before access.
